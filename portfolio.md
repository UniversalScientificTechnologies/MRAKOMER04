# MRAKOMĚR04

Open-source cloud cover sensor designed for astronomical observatories and meteorological stations. The device performs dual-zone infrared sky temperature measurement to detect cloud coverage.

## Parameters

 * Temperature measurement accuracy 1 °C
 * Dual zone measurement in FOV 40° for each zone
 * Measurement period 1 second
 * Measurement temperature range -65 to +40 °C
 * Operating temperature range -30 to +40 °C
